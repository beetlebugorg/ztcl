simdjson

The JSON parser of ztcl (src/json/) implements the simdjson algorithm
in Zig. simdjson is at https://github.com/simdjson/simdjson. Its license
is the Apache License 2.0 or the MIT license, at the choice of the user.

ztcl copies no code, no lookup table, and no constant set from
simdjson. The Zig code is new, written from the descriptions of the
method in these papers:

- Geoff Langdale and Daniel Lemire, "Parsing Gigabytes of JSON per
  Second", The VLDB Journal 28 (2019).
- John Keiser and Daniel Lemire, "Validating UTF-8 In Less Than One
  Instruction Per Byte", Software: Practice and Experience 51 (2021).
- Daniel Lemire, "Number Parsing at a Gigabyte per Second", Software:
  Practice and Experience 51 (2021). ztcl uses the Eisel-Lemire method
  through std.fmt.parseFloat of the Zig standard library.

docs/references/stdlib.md says what each part of src/json/ takes from
each source.
